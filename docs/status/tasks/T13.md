### Failures found and corrected

- The narrower image tag was unavailable; the official major-version image was
  resolved and pinned by digest. No unpinned production broker dependency added.
- Initial synthetic certificates lacked identifiers required by Python 3.14's
  strict verifier. Added SKI/AKI to fixtures, retaining strict verification. A
  TLS-context adapter closes/bounds failed handshakes around Paho 2.1 behavior.
- Docker automatically published ports changed across restart. The fixture now
  explicitly binds its selected loopback port and uses a per-test persistent
  client ID, stable across that test's worker restart, isolating MQTT sessions.
- Mosquitto file ACLs can grant a broad SUBSCRIBE while filtering deliveries.
  Tests now assert actual unauthorized delivery isolation with positive controls;
  they do not claim HiveMQ's subscription/retained controls were exercised.
- An intermittent broker-thread hang prompted single-network-owner Paho access
  and a bounded committed-completion queue. Focused and final complete checks pass
  after that correction. Hung test processes were explicitly stopped and only
  their generated orphan schemas/containers removed; existing services/data were
  preserved. Diagnostic timeout snapshots are observations, not skipped tests.

Known Starlette/httpx deprecation remains non-fatal. The account provider-sign-out
warning is deliberate fault injection. No assertion or TLS check was suppressed.
